The curve never changes when you switch models. Retrograde motion alone cannot distinguish these three geometries. The red section is where Mars appears to slip backward relative to the fixed stars.

01 / Geocentric
A loop added to a circle

A deferent and an epicycle reconstruct the reversal with Earth fixed. This simplified construction is not the full historical Ptolemaic model.

02 / Heliocentric
A change of vantage point

Earth travels faster than Mars around the Sun; the changing viewing direction naturally reverses near opposition.

03 / Tychonic
One sky, two frames

Earth stays still while the Sun carries Mars around it. In this circular construction, the apparent positions are identical.

About this model. A deliberately simplified, two-dimensional, circular-orbit demonstration with orbital periods of 365.25 and 686.98 days and a Mars/Earth orbital-radius ratio of 1.52. It shows the geometry of retrograde motion, not an ephemeris or a faithful reconstruction of Ptolemy's equant and eccentric circles. Real orbits are elliptical. Other evidence and physical dynamics distinguish modern heliocentrism from Earth-fixed cosmologies.
